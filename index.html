About Jerome

Clear guidance from someone who understands both the home and the financing.

I’ve always been fascinated by homes—their design, architecture, and the stories behind them. That curiosity led me into real estate, where I discovered how rewarding it is to help people make one of life’s biggest decisions.

Later, I became a Mortgage Loan Originator because I wanted to understand the financing side more deeply. Today, I help clients connect the real estate strategy with the mortgage strategy, so the path from planning to closing feels more informed and less stressful.
